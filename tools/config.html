washhouse configsample

Load a behaviour document

The washhouse namespace on the config service, or the sample (testdata/behaviour.example.json, the plan's values). Paste its JSON, or open a file. Nothing is sent anywhere.

—

    The definitions follow washhouse's decoder (internal/behaviour) and the validators of the packages each key feeds; a test fails when they drift. washhouse refuses the whole document for any problem shown here: a reload keeps the running config, a restart won't start. Changing a key marked "restart" needs washhouse restarted; every other change takes effect on a reload (SIGHUP).
